Our official stance has always been one of innovation and market leadership, but the true aim has always been total economic integration. The slow but deliberate transition from physical currencies to ECoin, our digital currency, is central to Project Colossus. As countries begin to adopt ECoin as a national standard and financial institutions rely on it for global transactions, we will exert control over the flow of capital with unparalleled precision.
Through subtle but strategic regulatory lobbying and economic coercion, we will make ECoin indispensable to the G20 nations, thus ensuring that all global trade and finance is anchored to our infrastructure.
Financial System Dependencies:

ECorp’s financial networks will replace legacy banking systems under the guise of security enhancements and cyber-resiliency initiatives. Once integrated, these systems will be designed to fail without our continual support. Every central bank, stock exchange, and multinational corporation will be tethered to us through dependencies so intricate that extracting ECorp from the equation would lead to financial collapse.
Our Quantum Financial Network (QFN), built with funding through our Dark Army partners, is nearly ready for deployment. This network will be unhackable and will render obsolete any financial systems not under our control.
2. Eliminating Competitors and Geopolitical Manipulation
For total dominance, competitors and geopolitical threats must be eliminated through both legal means and subversive operations. This is where our shadow alliances come into play.

Takedown of Rivals:
Rival Corporations: Our corporate sabotage teams have already infiltrated several major technology and banking firms that pose a threat to ECorp's control. National Governments: The reliance of national governments on ECorp’s infrastructure puts them in a delicate position. 3. Control of Energy Markets
Energy is the lifeblood of the global economy. By controlling the flow of energy, we control the flow of money. Project Colossus incorporates a three-pronged attack on global energy markets.

Phase 1: Renewable Energy Takeover:
We have been strategically acquiring renewable energy companies under a series of shell corporations. The public sees these moves as environmentally friendly, but the real purpose is to establish ECorp as the sole provider of renewable energy. We’ll control both the technology and distribution channels, forcing governments to comply with our policies under the banner of “sustainability.”
Phase 2: Energy Trade Manipulation:
ECorp already controls a significant portion of fossil fuel trading through our subsidiaries. Through collusion with key OPEC members and influence over energy futures markets, we are in a position to orchestrate energy price shocks that will devastate competitors and disrupt national economies. Our recent acquisition of critical rare earth mineral supplies has only further consolidated this power.
Phase 3: Energy Blackouts:
In targeted regions where resistance to ECorp's policies remains, we will leverage our control over energy infrastructure to cripple the grid through calculated blackouts and supply disruptions. These “accidents” will drive political leaders to their knees, forcing their dependence on our solutions.

Disinformation Campaigns:
